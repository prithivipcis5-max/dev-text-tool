DevText - Privacy-Focused Text & Developer Utility Suite

DevText is an all-in-one suite designed for programmers, content creators, students, and copywriters who require quick, reliable text processing utilities. All computations take place entirely client-side using JavaScript, ensuring your raw data, proprietary code, or confidential text is never sent to remote servers.

Features & Capabilities:

  • JSON Formatter & Validator: Detect syntax errors in raw JSON API payloads, beautify indented strings, or minify code for deployment.
  • Word & Character Counter: Analyze exact character counts, space metrics, and paragraph totals in real time for SEO and academic assignments.
  • Text Case Converter: Instantly switch strings between UPPERCASE, lowercase, and Title Case with one click.
  • Duplicate Line Cleaner: Filter unique text datasets by automatically identifying and removing duplicate lines.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):

Q: Is my data safe on DevText?
A: Yes. All data operations are executed inside your web browser. No inputs are stored or transmitted anywhere.

Q: Is DevText completely free?
A: Yes. DevText is 100% free to use, supported by unobtrusive display advertisements and user contributions.
